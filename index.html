About GeneTrace

Version: GeneTrace v1.0.0 (Release Candidate)

"From Sequence to Biological Meaning"

🔒 Privacy & Telemetry Model:
All sequence computations, translation, ORF detection, and statistics execute 100% client-side in this browser. Zero sequence data or analytics are transmitted to any server. External queries (NCBI BLAST) occur only when you explicitly click external links.
⚠️ Scientific Scope & Disclaimer:
GeneTrace is intended for education, research, and preliminary bioinformatics analysis. It is not designed or certified for clinical diagnosis, medical treatment, or regulatory filings.
